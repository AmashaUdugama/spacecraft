# Style Dataset Attribution

Images in modern/, minimalist/, industrial/, scandinavian/ (396 total, single-label
only) are sourced and filtered from:

Roboflow Universe - "Interiordesign Computer Vision Dataset" by class-qq9at
https://universe.roboflow.com/class-qq9at/interiordesign
License: CC BY 4.0 - cite this in your project's references.

Images were sorted into class folders using the dataset's official
_classes.csv ground-truth labels. Multi-labeled images (309 of 1,737) were
excluded to keep single-label training data clean.
